SHOOTING STARS

YOUR GUIDE TO THE COSMOS

An unpredictable universe. A fearless little traveller. Every jump brings a new challenge.

HOW TO PLAY

Use the arrow keys to move and jump. Left Arrow – move left. Right Arrow – move right. Up Arrow – jump. Down Arrow – bend or crouch.

ENERGY SYSTEM

Keep an eye on your energy. Collect coins to recover power and stay ready for the challenges ahead.

GOLDEN COINS

Collect glowing coins to increase your score and restore energy. Every 10 coins grant a protective shield.

A MOMENT OF PROTECTION

Earn a shield after collecting 10 coins. It activates automatically, lasts a few seconds, and protects you from hazards.

THINK YOU CAN CATCH ME?

The mischievous Rage Bait Coin appears briefly, moves erratically, and offers a bonus when collected quickly.

DANGERS OF THE COSMOS

Avoid falling meteors, asteroids, and other hazardous objects. Use your shield to survive close encounters.

SOUNDTRACK & COSMIC ATMOSPHERE

Enjoy the retro‑styled soundtrack and sound effects that react to your actions. Mute can be toggled with the speaker button.

BEHIND THE STARS

Shooting Stars is a neon‑lit space‑survival adventure where every jump is a risk, every coin is a chance, and the sky never stops moving.
